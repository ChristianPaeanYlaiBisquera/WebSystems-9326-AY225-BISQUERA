Featured Programs

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Our flagship program focuses on algorithms, software development, and computational theory. Students gain expertise in programming, data structures, and software engineering.

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

Focuses on IT infrastructure, network administration, cybersecurity, and system integration. Prepares students for careers in IT management and technical support.

Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence

Cutting-edge program covering machine learning, neural networks, natural language processing, and robotics. Prepares students for careers in AI research and development.

About the College

Our Mission

To provide excellent education in computer science and information technology through innovative teaching, cutting-edge research, and community engagement.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in computer science education and research, producing graduates who are globally competitive and socially responsible.

History

Established in 1990, the College of Computer Studies has been at the forefront of technology education for over 30 years. We have produced thousands of successful graduates who now lead in various tech industries worldwide.

Quality Education

We provide industry-relevant curriculum taught by experienced faculty.

Research Excellence

Our faculty and students engage in cutting-edge research projects.

Industry Partnerships

Strong connections with tech companies for internships and employment.
