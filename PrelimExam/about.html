About the College

Our Mission

To provide excellent education in computer science and information technology through innovative teaching, cutting-edge research, and community engagement. We aim to develop globally competitive graduates who are technically proficient, ethically grounded, and socially responsible.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in computer science education and research, recognized nationally and internationally for producing graduates who are innovators, leaders, and problem-solvers in the field of technology.

History

Established in 1990, the College of Computer Studies has been at the forefront of technology education for over 30 years. Starting with just 50 students and 5 faculty members, we have grown into a premier institution with over 1,500 students and 85 faculty members.

Throughout our history, we have maintained our commitment to excellence, innovation, and community service. Our alumni now hold leadership positions in major tech companies, research institutions, and startups worldwide.

Quality Education

We provide industry-relevant curriculum taught by experienced faculty using modern teaching methodologies and state-of-the-art facilities.

Research Excellence

Our faculty and students engage in cutting-edge research projects in AI, cybersecurity, data science, and software engineering.

Industry Partnerships

Strong connections with leading tech companies for internships, research collaborations, and employment opportunities.

Student-Centered

We prioritize student success through mentoring, career guidance, and extracurricular activities that enhance learning.
